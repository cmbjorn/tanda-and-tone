Privacy policy

Tanda & Tone · Last updated: 10 November 2026

In short

  • It collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere. No account, no analytics, no tracking.
  • It plays only the music files you choose, and never changes them.
  • It never records sound: only settings, your library's tags, numbers about each track and the set lists, on your Mac.
  • To remove everything, quit the app and delete its folder.

Tanda & Tone does not collect your data. It does not send anything anywhere: the app makes no network connections of its own and has no analytics, advertising or tracking. It has no account and no website form.

What the app can and cannot reach

It plays the music files you choose. It does not record sound from other apps, the system or a microphone, it does not talk to Spotify, Music or any other app, and it asks macOS for no permission to do so. It reads only the folders and files you give it through the open and save panels, and writes only where you say.

Purchases

The free trial and the unlock are bought through Apple's App Store, which handles the payment. The app only asks the App Store whether this Apple ID has them; we receive no personal or payment data.

What the app stores, and where

Everything is stored on your Mac, in the app's own folder, and never leaves it:

  • your settings (the output devices, the tone controls, the cortina folder and its settings, the pause between tracks, the columns and the lock);
  • the music folders you choose and, for each file in them, where it is, its tags (title, artist, orchestra, singer, rhythm, year and so on, with the corrections you make; your files themselves are never changed), its tempo and a few numbers about its loudness and tone, library.json;
  • for each track, a few numbers about its loudness and tone and your choices for it, so the app can level it better the next time, together with the track's title and artist;
  • your playlists, what is up next, and where you were when the app last closed, so it can offer to go on after a crash;
  • with the Restore Workshop: its list of recordings and, for each one it restored, a report of numbers;
  • the set log: for each evening, the tracks you played with the time, orchestra, singer, title, year and rhythm, as a text file and a playlist file in the sets folder, written as the evening goes;
  • logs for troubleshooting: numbers (loudness, performance) and the title and artist of the tracks played.

What it does not store

It never records sound. The only audio it holds is a few seconds of the track being played, in memory, to process it, and it is gone when the app stops. The Restore Workshop writes restored copies of your own files, the ones you give it, into the folder you choose, and nowhere else. Its trained denoise model is part of the app and runs on your Mac (the Neural Engine); no sound is sent anywhere to be processed.

Deleting your data

Quit the app, then delete its folder. In the version from the App Store it is ~/Library/Containers/com.christianmelbybjoern.tango/Data/Library/Application Support/Tanda & Tone/. That removes the library, the track numbers, the set logs and the logs in one step. Setup → Delete all notes removes only the per-track numbers. Restored copies and exported set lists are ordinary files in the folders you chose: delete them in Finder. Removing the app does not touch your music.

TestFlight

If you test a beta through Apple's TestFlight, any feedback you send (a screenshot, a comment, a crash report) goes to us through TestFlight under Apple's terms and privacy policy; the app itself sends nothing. If you write to us by email, we use your address only to answer you.

Children

The app is not aimed at children and collects nothing from anyone.
